## Closed-book review prompts

- What is the requirement or failure layer?
- Which identity is making the failing request?
- Which service/control supplies the missing capability?
- Why does the tempting alternative fail?
- What observation would prove the fix worked?

A topic is ready for final revision when you can explain it the next day using a new example.
